# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and inclusive project for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Assume good intent while communicating technical risk clearly.
- Welcome questions and help contributors learn the project.
- Give credit, protect private information, and accept responsibility for
  mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, personal attacks, doxxing, or retaliation are not tolerated in any
project space or when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy, investigate promptly, and
take proportionate action, including warnings, temporary restrictions, or a
permanent ban. Reports made in bad faith may also be acted upon.

This policy applies to repositories, reviews, issues, discussions, community
events, and other spaces where someone represents TMR.
